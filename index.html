        How it works

        Results model
        Claude rates every team's attack and defence from its scores this season and last, with recent games counting more, plus each league's home advantage. That gives each game's expected goals, and the chances of a home win, draw and away win follow from how often those goal counts end each way. Tested on 2,318 past games, each predicted only from the games before it, the top pick came in 50% of the time, just as the chances said. Bookmakers' odds are never used.
        Choosing doubles
        It tries every mix of singles, doubles and triples that fits your lines and keeps the one most likely to get every game right. Doubles go to the closest games, where a second outcome adds the most.
        Reading ×1.62
        Beside a single, "Doubling ×1.62" means a double there would make the whole ticket 1.62 times as likely to win, for twice the lines.
        Team news
        Each game has form, head-to-head and injury ratings, filled in by research or by you, on top of the results model. On a typical game, one step moves the home or away chance by about 4 points for form or injuries and 2 for head-to-head. A game without a results model also has a Strength rating, for the gap between the two teams, worth about 8 points a step. Set Team news weight to Off to see the results model alone.
        Research
        Send your jackpot screenshot to Claude in the chat. Claude works out each game's expected goals from the latest results, reads recent previews, injury lists and head-to-head records, sets the ratings with a note and sources for each, and adds the coupon to this page. Previews can be wrong or out of date, so read the notes.
        Track record
        Each tracked coupon keeps the chances the calculator gave, from the results model alone and with team news. Once results are in, it compares how often the top picks came in with how often the chances said they would, and scores both with the Brier score: lower is better, and calling every game a third each scores 0.667. A few weekends swing a lot by luck, so judge team news only after 40 or more games.
        Learning from results
        The calculator checks its chances against the results of your tracked coupons and learns three corrections: whether its favourites win more or less often than it said, whether draws do, and how much team news is really worth. Each one starts at no change and moves only as far as the results clearly support, so a lucky or unlucky weekend can't throw it off. Untick Learn from past results to see the chances without them.
        Coupon odds
        You can type in the coupon's odds, or a screenshot fills them in, but they play no part in the chances or the picks. They are only kept with a tracked coupon so the Monday report can show, after the games, how the bookmakers would have done by comparison.
        Your own judgement
        Change any rating under Team news, or click the percentage boxes to change a pick. The ticket shows what your version costs and how it compares.
        Screenshot import
        Claude reads the team names, kick-off times and any odds from your screenshot, using your own Claude account. It can misread, so check the teams.
        Lines and cost
        This assumes your jackpot charges per combination. If it prices doubles differently, use the chances and ignore the stake.
